Privacy demo uses real AES-GCM encryption in the browser with a random session key. The outside receiver tries a different key. Backup delivery assumes a separate, unaffected link with zero simulated loss; it is not a hardware guarantee. “Block both links” demonstrates a common outage. Encryption does not hide radio signals or radar reflections. Original hardware firmware is unchanged.

The plane moves along its route. Each second, a GPS/altitude telemetry packet travels toward your receiver. Turn the virtual jammer on to block some or all packets.

Loss and positions are illustrative. This scene does not model radar invisibility, RF radiation, calibrated range or an aircraft failsafe. Authentication checks are demonstrated in the packet demo; wiring and component faults are in the hardware lab.
