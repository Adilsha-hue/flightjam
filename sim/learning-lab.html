Physical model: two bench enclosures

Sender enclosure

USB data/power inlet → Heltec ESP32-S3/SX1262 board on insulating standoffs. Use mock telemetry first. Optional sensors are a later addition.

Receiver enclosure

Matching Heltec board → USB data/power outlet → laptop logger. Leave access to reset and boot buttons; keep metal fasteners away from exposed contacts.

Concept layout only: measure your exact board, connector positions and mounting holes before making a CAD enclosure. A physical RF evaluation harness requires separate lab engineering and validation.
