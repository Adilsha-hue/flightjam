Frame = ver(1) | dev(1) | seq(4) | ts(4) | plen(2) | payload(17) | CRC16(2) | HMAC-SHA256[:16] · header 12 B, overhead 30 B.
CRC16 is computed for real; HMAC is a real HMAC-SHA256 over a per-device PSK (the firmware uses mbedTLS on the ESP32-S3). Status is chosen by the RF model, then proven by decoding the bytes above. Reproduce offline: python3 host/sim/spectra_sim.py --selftest
