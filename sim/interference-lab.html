How a resilient link is built

Detect

Watch link-quality and error-rate indicators so a disturbance is noticed quickly. A detected drop is what lets mitigation engage automatically instead of silently losing telemetry.

Survive

Frequency agility and error-correcting coding keep data flowing through partial interference. Retransmission trades latency for delivery when a frame is missed.

Trust

Even under heavy disturbance, authentication and freshness checks stop altered or replayed frames from being accepted (see the Learning lab).

Recover

Fallback behavior matters: hold the last verified state, alarm on staleness, and restore full delivery as soon as the disturbance clears.
