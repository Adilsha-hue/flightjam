Conducted bench rig loaded. Nothing radiates: the interferer runs into a combiner and attenuator, not an antenna.
What this is: a Tinkercad-style drag-and-drop layout tool for the board under test and its conducted interferer test rig — the controller, the 868 MHz RF source, a step attenuator, a 2-way combiner, the DUT telemetry board and an optional inline monitor. Faults are wired through cables and pads.
What this is not: no RF amplifier, radiating antenna, build instructions, tuning or real-world power/frequency values are produced. Levels and behaviour are illustrative and match the repository's bench model only. This is a local workbench, not an Autodesk Tinkercad project.
