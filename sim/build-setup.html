Antenna selection

The antenna and radio-board band must match. Do not buy a 433 MHz antenna for the 866.5 MHz reference or assume changing an antenna converts a different board variant. Confirm the actual RF connector before selecting a pigtail. A simple antenna is sufficient for a telemetry bench design; claimed range needs measurements. Attach the appropriate antenna or lab termination before transmitting. GNSS needs its own compatible antenna if the chosen module does not include one.
